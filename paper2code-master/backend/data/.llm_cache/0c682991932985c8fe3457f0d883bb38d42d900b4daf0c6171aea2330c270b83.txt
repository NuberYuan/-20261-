The experiments show that transfer learning consistently outperformed training from scratch for both AlexNet and GoogLeNet across all image types and train/test splits. Performance generally improved as the training fraction increased, with the best result achieved by GoogLeNet using transfer learning on color images at an 80/20 split, yielding a mean F1 score of 0.9934. Color and segmented images tended to produce higher F1, precision, recall, and accuracy than grayscale images. The models were optimized using Stochastic Gradient Descent with a base learning rate of 0.005, a step learning rate decay by a factor of 10, and momentum of 0.9.
