The experimental results demonstrate that transfer learning significantly outperforms training from scratch for both AlexNet and GoogLeNet across all image types and data splits. GoogLeNet with transfer learning achieved the highest overall performance, with a mean F1 score of 0.9934 using color images at an 80/20 train/test split. Performance consistently improved with larger training datasets, and color images yielded better results than grayscale or segmented images across all configurations. The training process utilized standard fine-tuning hyperparameters, including Stochastic Gradient Descent with a base learning rate of 0.005, step decay learning rate policy, and momentum of 0.9, which proved effective for optimizing the deep learning models in this plant disease detection task.
